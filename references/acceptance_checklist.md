# Acceptance checklist

Use this file as the final validation rubric for `chinese-research-writing`.

## 1. Scope fit

- The requested task is substantial Chinese writing rather than a short casual reply.
- The chosen genre matches the request: thesis-style, report, article, commentary, or news/explanatory writing.
- The chosen output level matches the request: `提纲版`, `初稿版`, or `定稿版`.

## 2. Research and evidence

- Web research was completed before drafting new long-form content.
- Time-sensitive facts, policies, names, dates, and numbers were verified rather than recalled from memory.
- Core claims are attributed or phrased conservatively.
- Established facts, analysis, and recommendations are clearly separated.
- Evidence gaps or unresolved disagreements are made explicit when they matter.

## 3. Structure and prose

- The article has a visible central question, thesis, or reporting focus.
- Section order matches the chosen genre and supports the argument.
- Paragraphs do more than list points: they explain, support, and transition.
- Repetitive filler and generic AI phrasing have been removed.
- Terminology, dates, names, and numbers are consistent throughout.

## 4. Deliverables

- The final deliverable exists as markdown file output, not only as chat text.
- Long-document splitting follows [markdown_delivery_rules.md](./markdown_delivery_rules.md) when the manuscript is large.
- File names are stable and sortable.
- If chapter splitting is used, a compiled `99-全文汇总.md` file is also present.
- Reference numbering stays consistent across split files and the compiled manuscript.

## 5. References

- The reference style is appropriate for the task, with GB/T 7714 as the default.
- Bibliographic metadata is included when verified.
- Missing metadata is omitted rather than invented.
- The final reference list is complete enough for the reader to trace major claims.

## 6. User materials

- Uploaded files, links, notes, or draft fragments were integrated into the final structure.
- Non-negotiable user points were preserved.
- Contradictions in user materials were reconciled or clearly flagged.

## 7. Ready-to-ship standard

Treat the output as ready only if all items below are true:

- The genre is correct.
- The evidence base is adequate for the strength of the claims.
- The markdown artifacts are complete.
- The prose reads like intentional Chinese writing rather than stitched summary text.
